The Co-Intervention Imperative

Why Simple Pre/Post Averages Fail

In standard FP&A reporting, initiatives are frequently evaluated in isolation. However, with 3,844 stores simultaneously experiencing multiple interventions, an unadjusted comparison will double-count sales gains. For instance, a store receiving both Multi-Price Point ($3–$5 merchandise) and a Dedicated Cashier in February 2026 will show heightened throughput. Attributing 100% of the basket lift to MPP or 100% of transaction speed to DC creates a distorted causal picture. Multivariate Causal Synthetic Controls with Stratified Donors isolate the true marginal dollar lift per initiative with 0% unexplained residual.

Analytical Timing Consideration

Initiative Timing & Launch Disambiguation Framework

Accounting for customer price discovery vs. discrete operational go-live milestones

Causal Isolation Methodology
Multi-Price Point (MPP): Soft Launch vs. Hard Launch (Only for MPP) Dual Launch Markers

Unlike single-day physical renovations, Multi-Price Point rollouts trigger early customer discovery. The econometric engine distinguishes two critical timing dates for each MPP store:

1. Soft Launch Date
Calculated: First Scan > $1.00 USD
Empirically identified from store POS registers when customers first purchase items priced over $1.00 ($3, $4, $5 items) as initial cases are stocked before full aisle conversion.
2. Hard Launch Date
Dollar Tree Operational Sign-Off
The official milestone date where Dollar Tree operations considers that store live as MPP—complete with 100% shelf strip compliance, endcap signage, and DM sign-off.
Analytical Consideration: Using only the hard launch date would treat the 2–4 week soft ramp (where customers experience initial price discovery) as pre-period, contaminating baseline sales. Soft launch marks the true pre-period cutoff.
Dedicated Cashier (DC) & Paint & Powder (P&P) Single DT Date Provided

In direct contrast to MPP, Dedicated Cashier and Paint & Powder are operational milestones evaluated on one single event date provided directly by Dollar Tree for each store:

Dedicated Cashier (DC): 1 Go-Live Date

Single date provided by Dollar Tree when second designated cashier staffing began (concentrated Feb 2026 deployment). Instant queue relief without customer discovery lead time.

Paint & Powder (P&P): 1 Completion Date

Single date provided by Dollar Tree upon completion of the overnight interior refresh. Store opens the next morning fully remodeled with zero comp closure days.

Causal Impact: Clean, discrete step-function interventions with no pre-event leakage or ambiguous customer discovery delays.

The Story of Algorithm Selection: Rigorous Triage

Why standard clustering failed and Latent Class Analysis was selected for executive sign-off

K-Means Clustering DISQUALIFIED
Failure Mode: Euclidean Distortion

Store features are inherently mixed: continuous (footfall, square footage), ordinal (shrink risk 1–4), and categorical (geographic retail zone 1–6, anchor tenant type). K-Means computes Euclidean distances, artificially warping distances on non-metric retail spaces and collapsing clusters into false spheres.

High Silhouette distortion, poor balance
Hierarchical Agglomerative DISQUALIFIED
Failure Mode: 7,041-Store Chaining Disaster

Hierarchical linkage suffered catastrophic single-link chaining across the 7,041 candidate stores. Outlier rural stores with extreme square footage continuously merged into giant heterogeneous clusters, destroying distinct archetype boundaries required for test/control matching.

Chaining collapse, uninterpretable dendrogram
Latent Class Analysis (LCA) WINNER
Optimal Bayesian Probabilities on Mixed Features

LCA models the probability distribution of observed variables conditional on an unobserved latent class. Perfectly handles categorical geographic zones and discrete shrink ratings without artificial scaling. Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC) achieved minimum at exactly K=6 archetypes.

Econometric Stratification Framework

Test & Control Store Splitting & Wave Pooling Engine

Cluster-isolated donor pools, wave aggregation under Cluster × Zone × Month_Year, and the 4 non-negotiable control exclusion rules.

Zero Cross-Cluster Leakage
Core Pooling Philosophy No 1:1 Match

A Control Must Come From the Test Store's Own Cluster

A control must come from the test store's own cluster, so it sells like the test store. There is no one-to-one match. All eligible cluster stores form one pool, shared by all test stores that launch around the same time. Eliminating artificial pair matching prevents severe idiosyncratic variance and maximizes synthetic control donor weights.

Architecture: Shared Cluster Donor Pool
Wave Generation & Sizing N ≥ 25 Threshold

Waves Under Cluster × Zone by Launch Month

Waves are created under Cluster × Zone based on Initiative launch months (Month_Year). Adaptive Month Combination Rule: We combine up to 3 consecutive months if a single month does not have enough test stores (< 25 stores) to preserve statistical degrees of freedom.
