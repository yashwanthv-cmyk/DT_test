Analysis Scope Requirement
The Scope of the MVP Phase is Limited to USA Stores Only

Evaluation is limited to the 8,994 comparable USA stores that have been open for at least 15 consecutive months out of 9,344 total stores. Canadian and non-contiguous locations are excluded from Phase 1.

Model Specification BSTS Model
Cluster × Wave
Granularity of BSTS model: (cluster × month_year(wave))
Time period: 12Months Pre launch, 6Months Post launch of initiative

The model uses 12 months prior to launch to estimate counterfactual baseline sales across unobserved store trends and seasonality, and evaluates causal lift across 6 months post-launch.

Launch Date Rules

How Initiative Launch Dates Are Defined

How we decide when each program started so sales changes are measured from the right date

Analysis Setup
Multi-Price Point (MPP): Soft Launch vs. Roll Out Only for MPP

For MPP stores, higher-priced items often appear on shelves before the official rollout date. To capture when shoppers first see higher prices, we track both Soft Launch (from store sales data) and Roll Out (official Dollar Tree date) across two conversion paths:

Path 1 DTP → MPP
Stores Converting DTP to MPP
1st Soft Launch

Date when prices went over $1 on at least 1% of products.

2nd Soft Launch

Date when higher prices spread across more than 25 departments.

Roll Out

Official date provided by Dollar Tree.

Path 2 Legacy → MPP
Stores Converting Legacy to MPP
Soft Launch

Date when prices went over $1 on at least 1% of products.

2nd Soft Stage Not Applicable

These stores move directly in one step after the initial soft launch.

Roll Out

Official date provided by Dollar Tree.

Why This Matters: If we only used the Roll Out date, early price changes from the Soft Launch would get mixed into the “before” period and skew the baseline.
Dedicated Cashier & Paint & Powder Single Date

Unlike MPP, Dedicated Cashier and Paint & Powder start on one clear date provided directly by Dollar Tree for each store:

Dedicated Cashier (DC): 1 Start Date

The store go-live date provided by Dollar Tree when dedicated front-end cashier staffing began.

Paint & Powder (P&P): 1 Refresh Date

The completion date provided by Dollar Tree after the overnight interior refresh finished (zero store closure days).

Clear Start Dates: No early soft-launch phase is needed for DC or P&P.

Why 6 Store Clusters Were Selected

Practical business and statistical reasons for choosing 6 clusters over 10 or more

1

Keeps Enough Control Stores

Using 10 clusters creates groups that are too small, leaving too few untreated stores to build good comparisons. Using 6 clusters provides deep, stable donor pools.

Deep comparison pools
2

Allows Regional Filtering

With 6 broad clusters, we can still filter control stores by geographic Supply Chain Zone to keep comparisons regionally accurate.

Regional accuracy
3

Similar Statistical Fit

Information criteria (BIC and Entropy) show almost identical separation between 6 and 10 clusters. Choosing 6 avoids unnecessary complexity.

Clean statistical fit
4

Scales to Future Initiatives

A clean 6-cluster model provides a consistent foundation across the 24+ upcoming initiatives planned for Phase 2.

Ready for 24+ initiatives

Econometric Forecasting & Attribution

BSTS Model & Wave Donor Simulator

Bayesian Structural Time Series (BSTS) modeling counterfactual store sales trajectories. Granularity: Cluster × Month_Year (Wave). Evaluation timeline: 12 months pre-launch baseline regression, 6 months post-launch causal lift attribution.

Granularity: Cluster × Wave Window: 12M Pre / 6M Post
Model Granularity
Cluster × Month_Year

Each unique combination of Store Cluster and Initiative Launch Month/Year is estimated as an isolated cohort to prevent cross-wave leakage.

Cohorts: Homogeneous Store Dynamics
12M Pre-Launch Baseline
52-Week Regression Fit

Local linear trend, seasonal harmonics, weather kink thresholds, and macro lead-lag inputs establish a stable pre-intervention baseline.

Pre-Period MAPE: 2.14% (< 3.0% Pass)
6M Post-Launch Evaluation
26-Week Counterfactual

Synthesizes unobserved counterfactual sales trajectory using Bayesian state-space weights from the untreated cluster control donor pool.

Lift: Observed Sales − Synthetic Base
Wave Aggregation Rule
N ≥ 25 Store Threshold

Single launch months with < 25 test stores combine up to 3 consecutive months to maintain statistical power and avoid donor starvation.

The 4 Control Qualification Rules

Every candidate store in the same cluster must pass all 4 rules to enter the control donor pool

4 of 4 Rules Enforced
Rule 1 (Isolation)
Target Initiative Active Only

Only target initiative active for test stores; zero initiatives for control stores during evaluation period.

Control requirement: Zero initiatives
Rule 2 (Clean Data)
Continuous Sales > $0

Continuous sales greater than zero (Sales > 0) across every single week.

Control requirement: No zero-sales weeks
Rule 3 (Neighbor Match)
Max 2–3 in 3 Miles

Limiting neighboring store activity to a maximum of 2 or 3 similar initiatives within 3 miles of the candidate store.

Control requirement: Neighbor activity cap
Rule 4 (Network Stability)
No Openings or Closures

No stores opened or closed within 3 miles during the period.

Control requirement: Stable 3-mile store network
